# Running a hub - and sending your first parcel

## Before you start

- You need Basecamp with `mulenet` installed (it pulls `mulenet_core` and `loam_core`).
- Pick a **handoff address** other hubs ship to: a parcel locker is best (no home address, no
  face). You'll grant it to hubs one by one; it's never published.
- Pick a **public intake** for senders: "at the monthly Circle meetup" or a public locker. Never
  your home.
- Get a few standard boxes in each class you accept (S 25x18x8, M 35x25x15, L 50x35x20), plain
  tape, filler for padding (paper, foam), and a kitchen scale.

## Set up (once)

1. **My hub** -> fill in name, city, two-letter country, what you carry, ship days, max hold,
   intake -> **Start my hub**.
2. **Directory** -> **Trust this steward** with your Circle steward's address (or vouch hubs
   yourself; you are your own trust root).
3. **My hub** -> **Who may ship to you** -> grant your handoff address to each hub you're willing
   to receive from.

## When a parcel arrives

1. Scan the label (or paste its text), type the **outer sleeve's seal code**, weigh the box ->
   **Check parcel**.
2. If it's refused (wrong seal, a category you don't carry, overweight) MuleNet tells you why and
   the sender is told too. Don't open it.
3. If it's accepted, MuleNet shows the steps:
   - remove the outer sleeve with that seal code; throw away the old box and label;
   - put the rest in a **fresh plain box** of the class shown, add the padding shown;
   - print the **new label** (Save label as SVG) and stick it on;
   - ship it to the address shown, on or after the ship day (that's the batching);
   - press **I handed it to the carrier**. The receipt goes out a few hours later.
4. When the next hub confirms receipt, MuleNet shows it as your alibi.

## Opsec checklist

- Plain boxes and plain tape only; no stickers, no handwriting, no company logos.
- Don't reuse the incoming box, filler or tape.
- Print labels on a plain printer; don't add notes.
- Ship on your batch days, not the day the parcel arrived.
- Never photograph or share anything about a parcel.
- Don't open sleeves. If something seems wrong, refuse it.

## Sending your first parcel (the 3D-printed test)

1. The recipient creates a mailbox (**Receive**) at the last hub and sends you the card.
2. **Send** -> what it is, category `printed-object`, box S, packed weight, 3 hops, drop-off hub,
   paste the card -> **Plan route and make the label**.
3. Pack: item in a plain bag (the core), then the sleeves from the packing list. The last one in
   the list goes on first; write each seal code on that sleeve's tamper sticker.
4. Box class S, label on, hand it over at the entry hub's intake.
5. Watch **Your parcels**: each step fills in as receipts arrive.
